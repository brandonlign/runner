The current flagship choice does not retroactively change any earlier PASS/FAIL token or frozen gate. In particular, the TopoModal sparse-recovery experiment's historical conjunctive verdict remains preserved. The flagship is selected for the directly demonstrated combination of:

1. substantially stronger cross-scale candidate identity under ~8x sample-size stress;
2. substantially higher known-stream recovery at identical candidate budget;
3. substantially higher candidate purity;
4. unchanged median fragmentation;
5. direct set-level superiority to ordinary HDBSCAN EOM on the exact sparse panels where ordinary and recurrent EOM selected identical candidate catalogues.
