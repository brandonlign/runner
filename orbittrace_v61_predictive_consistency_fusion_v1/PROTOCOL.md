## Exact transferred predictive feature

For each route independently and for each fixed family:

1. Split the immutable family membership into 2013 and 2014 using the canonical label-free event lookup. Every family must have at least one member in each year or execution is technically invalid.
2. For each year, define `center_sol` as the circular mean of the annual member solar longitudes. This is the same solar-longitude centering rule used by the GMN family centroid construction that supplied the diagnostic predictor.
3. Normalize each event to solar longitude, solar-centered radiant longitude, ecliptic latitude, and positive geocentric speed.
4. If annual membership has at least four events, perform deterministic leave-one-out prediction. For each held-out event, fit ordinary least squares on all remaining annual members with design `[1, signed_delta_solar_longitude / 10 deg]` and response `[radiant unit-vector x, y, z, log(vg)]`.
5. Normalize the predicted radiant vector to unit length and evaluate the held-out event with the exact fixed physical residual `hypot(radiant_angle / 3 deg, abs(delta_log_vg) / log(1.08))`.
6. If annual membership has fewer than four events, use the exact diagnostic fallback: static normalized mean radiant unit vector plus mean log(vg), evaluated with the same residual. No family is deleted.
7. Summarize the family by worst-year predictive q90, worst-year predictive median, worst-event predictive residual, worst-year static q90, q90 gain (`static_q90 - predictive_q90`), and learned-member fraction.

The **sole predictive order** is:

`(lower worst-year predictive q90, lower worst-year predictive median, higher q90 gain, family_id)`.
