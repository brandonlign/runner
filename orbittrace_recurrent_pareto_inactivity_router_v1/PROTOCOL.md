# OrbitTrace recurrent-Pareto inactivity router v1

## Scientific question

Can the established small-survey failure of recurrent-EOM be mitigated without changing recurrent-EOM where its recurrence mechanism is genuinely active?

The router is a panel-level structural composition of two already-frozen methods. It introduces no learned score, threshold, sample-size cutoff, truth-informed switch, or external-survey calibration.

## Motivation frozen before router truth

Zero-label GMN scale-stress run `31929171717` / artifact `9258799549` established that fixed-support recurrent-EOM becomes increasingly identical to ordinary HDBSCAN EOM as sample size falls. Exact frozen mechanism activity on the deterministic scale-stress panels is:

- d=64: bucket 0 ACTIVE; bucket 1 INACTIVE; bucket 2 INACTIVE; bucket 3 ACTIVE.
- d=128: buckets 0,1,2,3 INACTIVE.
- d=1024: buckets 0,1,2,3 INACTIVE.

`ACTIVE` means recurrent-EOM and ordinary EOM select different condensed-tree node sets. `INACTIVE` means the selected-node sets are exactly identical. This is label-free and was established before this router protocol.

The already-frozen recurrent-TopoModal Pareto-prominence method is a 10/10 PASS on d=128/d=1024. Its exact direct d=64 translation is closed because bucket 0 contains a TopoModal child overlapping multiple recurrent parents. Importantly, bucket 0 is an ACTIVE recurrent-EOM panel and therefore the router defined here never invokes Pareto there. No ambiguity rescue is authorized.

## Exact routing rule

For every deterministic `(denominator,bucket)` panel:

1. Reconstruct or rebind the exact ordinary-EOM and recurrent-EOM selected-node identities under the frozen scale-stress configuration.
2. If the selected-node sets differ, emit exact recurrent-EOM candidates and exact recurrent-EOM order. No Pareto construction is attempted or inspected for that panel.
3. If the selected-node sets are exactly identical, emit the exact already-frozen Pareto-prominence candidate construction/order:
   - fixed-scale support-resolved TopoModal children;
   - retain a child iff it overlaps exactly one recurrent parent;
   - abort if any support child overlaps more than one recurrent parent;
   - rank retained children by the exact original Pareto-prominence rule using minimizing corroborating recurrent-parent rank and modal-prominence rank;
   - equal evaluation budget is the recurrent parent candidate count K.
4. No fallback exists if an INACTIVE panel cannot construct the exact original Pareto catalogue with at least K candidates. Such a panel is a binding PRETRUTH FAIL and truth remains unopened.

There is no `n` threshold and no denominator-specific scientific exception. Mechanism activity alone controls routing.

## Pretruth authorization

Before shower truth may open, the router must prove all of the following:

1. protected `[20°,55°]` exclusion is intact;
2. exact 12-panel scale-stress universes reproduce;
3. exact mechanism-activity map above reproduces;
4. active-panel routed outputs are byte-equivalent in membership/order to recurrent-EOM;
5. d=128/d=1024 inactive outputs are byte-equivalent to the frozen successful Pareto prelabel;
6. d=64 buckets 1 and 2 satisfy exact unique-parent corroboration without rescue;
7. d=64 buckets 1 and 2 each have Pareto capacity at least recurrent K;
8. all routed catalogues are pairwise-disjoint and contain only panel event IDs;
9. no shower truth or external scientific data were accessed.

Failure of any condition closes this exact router before truth.

## Binding target-excluded GMN scale evaluation

Only after a fully sealed pretruth PASS, evaluate exact routed catalogue versus exact recurrent-EOM at equal K on all 24 annual panels: 3 denominators x 4 buckets x 2 years.

Primary metrics per panel are:
- qualified/recovered shower count;
- zero-filled eligible-query MRR;
- top-100 dominant precision;
- median top-500 fragmentation;
- recovered@25, @50, @100 and @500 for diagnosis.

Historical recovered-only conditional MRR is diagnostic only.

For each scale d=64,128,1024, the router must satisfy four no-regression gates versus recurrent-EOM:
- pooled qualified total not lower;
- mean zero-filled MRR not lower;
- mean top-100 dominant precision not lower;
- mean fragmentation not higher.

Additionally, d=64 must show at least one strict material effect: either pooled qualified total strictly greater OR mean zero-filled MRR strictly greater. This prevents an entirely inactive router from passing merely by identity.

All 13 gates are mandatory.

## Interpretation boundary

A PASS would demonstrate sample-size robustness/generalization across the frozen GMN thinning range while preserving recurrent-EOM unchanged on panels where recurrence is active. It would not erase or rerun the pristine NASA ASFN 2018/2019 negative result and would not by itself establish pristine cross-survey validation.

A FAIL closes this exact panel-level mechanism-inactivity router. No activity tolerance, node-overlap threshold, sample-size threshold, fallback hierarchy, alternate Pareto correspondence, parent-set resolver, route-specific exception, or post-result rescue is authorized.
