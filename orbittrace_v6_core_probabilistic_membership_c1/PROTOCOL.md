## Status and succession

Protocol-only freeze created before the current fanout-v2 v6 development execution has produced a scientific result and before any v6 matched-literature result exists. No C1 science, target-region event, OrbitTrace target information, or literature outcome is accessed here.

C1 is the designated successor only for this specific future outcome:

1. exact repaired v3-primary catalogue v6 **passes** its target-excluded GMN 2022/2023 development gates; and
2. the already-frozen matched Sugar/HDBSCAN adjudication then returns `NO_LITERATURE_SUPERIORITY`.

That outcome would mean the v6 detector/family construction remains scientifically viable while its complete catalogue performance is insufficient. C1 therefore keeps the exact v6 detector, recurrent-family universe, and primary rank unchanged and changes only post-core membership.

If v6 fails development, C1 is not authorized; the separately frozen v8-core P1 path remains the successor. If v6 establishes `SPARSE_STREAM_SUPERIORITY` or `BROAD_CATALOGUE_SUPERIORITY`, C1 is not authorized because no development modification is needed.

This succession is fixed before the v6 result so no choice between v8-core P1 and v6-core C1 can be made from whichever later looks better.

## Why C1 is distinct from P1

P1 is a membership architecture around the exact promoted-v8 226-family core universe. C1 uses the exact primary recurrent-family output of repaired catalogue v6 instead. No v8 family may replace, seed, merge, rank, or rescue a v6 primary family in C1.

The numerical membership model is deliberately inherited unchanged from the already-frozen P1 architecture rather than searched again. This isolates the scientific question: whether better v6 core discovery plus a conservative probabilistic membership layer is sufficient to close the catalogue-F1 gap.

The v6 fixed4 rescue queue remains a separate reported queue and is not eligible to seed C1, alter the primary family universe, or satisfy a C1 gate.

## Membership model

For each exact v6 primary recurrent family:

1. Treat its original v6 event union as immutable seeds.
2. For each year separately, compute the seed centroid with the same robust/circular conventions frozen for P1: circular mean solar longitude, circular mean Sun-centered longitude, median ecliptic latitude, and median geocentric speed.
3. Express seeds and candidate events in the same four normalized observation coordinates used by P1: wrapped solar-longitude residual / 4°, wrapped Sun-centered-longitude residual / 2° with the latitude cosine correction, ecliptic-latitude residual / 2°, and geocentric-speed residual / 2 km/s.
4. Pool seed-only residuals across the two years after their year-specific centering and fit exactly one Oracle Approximating Shrinkage covariance per family. Added members never enter this fit.
5. The candidate stream region is exactly the 99% four-dimensional chi-square ellipsoid.
6. The local-background shell is exactly the 99%–99.99% four-dimensional chi-square annulus.
7. Estimate local background with the exact one-sided 95% Garwood Poisson upper mean from shell counts.
8. For each year, estimate stream amplitude as immutable current-year seed count plus only positive non-seed excess over conservative expected background inside the 99% ellipsoid, divided by 0.99 containment.
9. Compute the Gaussian stream intensity for every non-seed candidate compatible with each family.
10. Original seeds never compete, move, disappear, or change family.
11. For a non-seed event compatible with multiple families, compete all stream intensities simultaneously against the conservative background intensity. Assign only to the maximum-intensity family when its normalized responsibility is strictly greater than 0.5.
12. Added events never seed another event, alter a centroid/covariance/background/amplitude, merge families, or trigger another growth iteration.

Thus the P1 probabilities, covariance estimator, shell, background confidence, responsibility threshold, and one-shot semantics are transplanted exactly; none may be retuned for v6.
