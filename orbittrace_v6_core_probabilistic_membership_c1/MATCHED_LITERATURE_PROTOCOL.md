1. C1 macro F1 >= comparator macro F1 + 0.05;
2. no nonempty size stratum has C1 mean F1 more than 0.05 below comparator;

4. C1 count of showers with F1 > 0.5 is not lower than comparator;
5. all common-universe/integrity/pre-truth gates pass.

## Sparse-stream superiority

If broad superiority does not pass, C1 may classify `SPARSE_STREAM_SUPERIORITY` only if **for each comparator independently** and in **both 2023 and 2025**:

1. 4–9 mean F1 >= comparator + 0.10;
2. combined 4–24 mean F1 >= comparator + 0.10;
3. macro F1 is no more than 0.10 below comparator;
4. C1 retains at least 80% of comparator's count of showers with F1 > 0.5;
5. all common-universe/integrity/pre-truth gates pass.

No averaging across HDBSCAN and Sugar can rescue a failed comparator panel.
