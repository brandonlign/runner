# Real-shower meta-learning boundary

## Proposed methodological core

Train an episodic permutation-equivariant detector on **real established shower member sets mixed with real local sporadic backgrounds**, so the model learns reusable stream morphology rather than hand-coded synthetic lines or individual shower identities.

Each training episode selects a shower and observing interval, downsamples a sparse subset of real members, mixes those members with nearby sporadics, and asks the model to predict both candidate presence and event membership. Evaluation holds out entire shower codes and related shower complexes.

## Why this is not yet a method

GMN shower labels are generated by an existing association system. They are noisy teacher labels and cannot be treated as independent discoveries. A neural model could appear successful by learning:

- solar-longitude windows;
- the existing association radius or shower lookup table;
- shower identity rather than transferable stream structure;
- only the morphology of strong cataloged showers.

## Required controls before continuation

1. Entire shower-code and related-complex holdouts.
2. Solar-longitude-only baseline and solar-longitude ablation.
3. Labeler-proxy baseline using established radiant/velocity windows.
4. Matched-track and density baselines.
5. Downsampled weak-member tests.
6. Untouched real weak-stream transfer outside the teacher labels.
7. No GhostStream data in training, model selection, threshold selection, or architecture decisions.

## Novelty boundary

The following are not novel: attention, set networks, episodic learning, noisy-label training, or meteor shower association.

A potential domain contribution exists only if real-shower episodic learning discovers transferable weak-stream morphology that materially outperforms the labeler proxy and conventional track/density methods on wholly unseen shower complexes and independent real weak streams.

No priority claim is allowed at the audit stage.
