## Comparator and information-parity rule

The benchmark will reproduce the already-frozen comparator definitions rather than choosing new favorable variants:

- **catalogue HDBSCAN:** same feature representation, clustering semantics, assignment output, and exact-row pairwise evaluation interface as the frozen SonotaCo 2023/2025 catalogue-HDBSCAN comparator;
- **Sugar:** same uncertainty-aware Sugar implementation, scientific parameters, assignment semantics, and exact-row pairwise evaluation interface as the frozen SonotaCo 2023/2025 Sugar comparator;
- any year-specific parser/schema adaptation must be source-audited as technical transport only before event values are read;
- comparator generation and successor checkpoint generation must use the same exact row IDs within each comparator/year panel;
- the solar-longitude interval 20°–55° must be removed before detector/scientific-value retention and before known-shower truth is indexed;
- comparator cluster values and known-shower truth remain inaccessible until the successor output for **both comparators and both years** is frozen and hashed.

A comparator that cannot be faithfully represented on 2014/2016 causes `ARCHITECTURE_INCOMPATIBLE_FRESH_MATCHED_PANEL`; it does not authorize a post-data substitute comparator, year, or weakened claim.

## Frozen superiority standard

The scientific advancement standard remains the exact P14/P18 sparse-superiority standard, separately against both comparators in both years:

- 4–9-member mean F1 gain >= +0.10;
- combined 4–24-member mean F1 gain >= +0.10;
- overall macro-F1 delta >= -0.10;
- retain at least 80% of that comparator's count of showers with F1 > 0.5;
- all common-universe, provenance, information-parity, integrity, and target-firewall gates pass.

Broad-only superiority is insufficient. Cross-comparator denominator mixing is forbidden.

## Successor-development boundary

SonotaCo 2014/2016 may not be used to choose or tune the successor architecture, features, thresholds, family construction, membership rule, score, ranking, or stopping rule. The successor must first be frozen from target-excluded development evidence and pass its own preregistered development gates.

If the currently executing pre-P18-frozen B1 architecture passes development, B1 may be transported to this panel under a separately source-audited, no-retuning adapter. If B1 fails, its failure is preserved and a genuinely new successor may be developed on target-excluded development data; this same 2014/2016 reservation remains unread and available to that later frozen successor.
