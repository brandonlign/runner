# Pre-binding diagnostic boundary

Before this 1,000-clone execution, exposed local audits at 100 and 260 uncertainty realizations had already shown that removing the fixed 23% Sugar percentile materially improves Sugar and that recurrent-EOM nevertheless led the calibrated reconstruction at fixed candidate budgets 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 in both 2013 and 2014. Those truncated runs are diagnostic only and do not determine the binding 1,000-clone result.

Because those diagnostics exist, the full run is explicitly corrective/exposed and cannot be described as a pristine preregistered superiority test. No parameter may be changed in response to the 1,000-clone outcome.
