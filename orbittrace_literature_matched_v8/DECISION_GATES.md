# Numeric comparison gates frozen before execution

These gates make the word **materially** in `PROTOCOL.md` quantitative before any new v8 SonotaCo result exists.

## Sparse-bin mean-F1 gates

For each published comparator and each year separately, define

`delta = v8 annual matched-family mean F1 - comparator annual mean F1`.

A bin is a **material v8 advantage** only when `delta >= 0.10`.

A comparator is **beaten in the 4–9 sparse regime on this benchmark** only if the 4–9 bin is a material v8 advantage in **both 2023 and 2025**.

A comparator is **beaten across the broader sparse regime** only if both the 4–9 and 10–24 bins are material v8 advantages in **both years**.

A one-year advantage, a delta below 0.10, or a bin with no v8 reference showers is reported descriptively and is not counted as a pass.

## Non-sparse reporting gate

The 25–49, 50–99, and 100+ bins are always reported. A sparse win is invalidated as a broad all-regime superiority claim if it is presented without those results.

## Claim gate

Even if the sparse gates pass, the allowed conclusion is limited to the SonotaCo target-excluded benchmark and the faithfully transferred methods actually tested. The words **state of the art** require an additional event-row-matched analysis or a clearly stated narrower formulation. This run by itself cannot establish superiority to CMOR wavelets because the wavelet comparator is deferred for input incompatibility, and it cannot turn the episode/targeted D_SH comparator into a catalogue-discovery method.
