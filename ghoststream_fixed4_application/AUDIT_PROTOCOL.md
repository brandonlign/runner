# Frozen GhostStream fixed-4° application source audit

## Purpose

Authorize one new validation phase that connects the already frozen fixed-4° coverage-normalized Mondrian anchored four-clique detector to the previously discovered GhostStream candidate.

This phase does **not** claim that the detector originally discovered GhostStream. It asks whether a separately developed and frozen method can independently recover or support the candidate when applied after all methodology choices are locked.

## Immutable detector

The only eligible detector source is the standalone final candidate with SHA-256:

`747b2b1471f3ba193d68a39dd82ad3ac8506be63b651d45f84ffabb8d1acd301`

Its relative-solar-longitude scale remains exactly 4° per distance unit. No score, feature scale, neighborhood size, calibration count, seed family, p-value threshold, support rule, or scientific gate may change.

## Immutable GhostStream evidence input

The canonical GhostStream artifact is runner artifact `8814798136`, whose ZIP SHA-256 is:

`716b70313465d5df4bfb092a85a81680e6f618606b71e25470c63c480b6449f5`

The application must use the exact event identities and geometry preserved in that artifact. It may not edit membership, remove inconvenient members, or refit the candidate center before scoring.

## Blinding sequence

1. Verify and decode the frozen detector before reading any GhostStream scientific value.
2. Freeze the application code, window construction, calibration streams, outputs, and interpretation rules in a separate execution PR.
3. Score the GMN data without using GhostStream identity in the detector or calibration.
4. Freeze all scores and candidate rankings.
5. Only then join the exact canonical GhostStream event identities and report recovery.

## Required execution outputs

The separately frozen execution must report:

- the detector p-value and raw score for every tested GhostStream-containing episode;
- whether p ≤ 0.05 and p ≤ 0.01;
- how many of the detector-selected quartet are canonical GhostStream members;
- candidate rank among identically constructed label-blind windows;
- results by year and pooled across eligible years;
- sensitivity to member count only through the already frozen k = 4, 6, 8, and 12 episode construction, without selecting the most favorable k after seeing GhostStream;
- a clear distinction between targeted independent validation and full catalogue-wide rediscovery.

## Interpretation boundary

- A significant, high-ranking recovery with a selected quartet dominated by canonical members connects the novel detector to GhostStream as an independent frozen validation.
- A high rank without nominal significance is partial support only.
- Failure to recover must be preserved and means the method cannot be presented as supporting GhostStream.
- No outcome authorizes post-result tuning or relabelling the detector as the original discovery method.

This audit reads no GhostStream scientific value and computes no GhostStream score.
