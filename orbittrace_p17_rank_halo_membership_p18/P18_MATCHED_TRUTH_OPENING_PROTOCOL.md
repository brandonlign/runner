# P18 matched-literature one-time truth-opening protocol

## Status

Frozen before any successful P17/P18 matched pretruth checkpoint and before any comparator cluster value or known-shower truth value is opened for P18. No external or target data are authorized.

P18 is already fixed as the sole primary matched challenger. P17 core-only membership is a diagnostic ablation only and cannot replace P18 after truth.

## Hard pretruth barrier

Truth opening is forbidden until immutable artifacts prove both HDBSCAN and Sugar P18 checkpoints were frozen with:

- years exactly `[2023, 2025]` and blind exclusion `[20.0, 55.0]`;
- `competitor_cluster_values_accessed=false` and `known_shower_truth_accessed=false`;
- exact P17 matched scientific source SHA256 `c0c39d1bd660efbe5e5353b5a33185428a6f60f4a3759be3acd16a15a063012a`;
- exact P18 checkpoint transform blob `3f53e1ffeabb1b32d2de124cbf903d193012b9af`;
- P18 primary family existence/rank inherited unchanged from P17/P14;
- P18 evaluator-facing membership frozen as the exact label-free P17/P15/P12 halo;
- every P17 reciprocal-closure ledger/hash frozen and fail-closed before truth;
- P18 core↔halo correspondence and membership hashes frozen before truth;
- no new detector/score/distance/threshold/family/proposal/growth/merge/rank operation in P18.

If either panel lacks a valid checkpoint, no truth value may be opened.

## Exact one-time evaluator chain

After the hard barrier, the truth-opening job may do only the following:

1. validate the exact immutable P18 HDBSCAN and Sugar checkpoint bytes/hashes;
2. stage the already-frozen matched SonotaCo 2023/2025 comparator inputs, common-truth denominator inputs, parser sources, raw archives, mapping audit, and evaluator sources used by the existing P13/P14 matched-literature lineage;
3. construct the P18-compatible P13 finalizer by the exact two-surface reversible compatibility patch frozen in `prepare_p18_compatible_p13_finalizer.py`;
4. run the already-frozen blind-safe matched evaluator **once** on the two immutable P18 checkpoints;
5. pass that raw evaluator result through the existing P14 transport/result finalizer chain and the frozen `finalize_p18_matched_result.py` provenance wrapper;
6. write one immutable P18 result artifact and its canonical hash.

No P17/P18 detector, halo proposal, family constructor, score, distance, membership model, threshold, merge, or ranking code may run after truth becomes available. The evaluator must consume the frozen checkpoint objects exactly as written.

## Frozen evaluator mathematics and promotion gate

P18 inherits the existing pairwise matched-data evaluator mathematics and denominators unchanged. The P18 compatibility layer may change only the already-audited transport provenance and the diagnostic assertion reflecting precommitted halo-membership output semantics.

The promotion decision is determined solely by the already-frozen sparse-superiority gates separately against both catalogue HDBSCAN and Sugar in both years:

- 4–9-member mean F1 gain >= +0.10;
- combined 4–24-member mean F1 gain >= +0.10;
- overall macro-F1 delta >= -0.10;
- retain at least 80% of comparator showers with F1 > 0.5;
- all common-universe, provenance, integrity, and firewall requirements pass.

`PASS_P18_MATCHED_SPARSE_SUPERIORITY_BOTH_COMPARATORS_BOTH_YEARS` is the only matched verdict that authorizes the separately preregistered no-retuning MAARSY test. Broad-only superiority does not authorize external validation.

Any pairwise sparse-gate failure produces `FAIL_P18_MATCHED_SPARSE_SUPERIORITY_NO_GO` and does not authorize external validation.

## No posttruth candidate/model selection

After truth is visible, the following are forbidden regardless of observed metrics:

- replacing P18 with P17 core-only membership;
- choosing core versus halo per family, rank, size bin, year, or comparator;
- changing any membership threshold or support floor;
- recomputing family rank;
- selecting a different comparator universe or truth denominator;
- changing the sparse-superiority gates;
- defining a new combined metric or rescue criterion;
- rerunning the detector after inspecting matched errors.

P17 core-only may be evaluated only as a preregistered diagnostic ablation using the same frozen truth opening, and its metrics cannot determine promotion.

## Firewall and claim boundary

The truth-opening job may not access MAARSY data, any external-validation result, any 20°–55° target-region discovery data, or any withheld target reference.

A matched P18 PASS establishes only superiority under the frozen SonotaCo matched benchmark and authorizes the separately frozen MAARSY 2020/2021 no-retuning test. It does not authorize target access.

This protocol must not be changed in response to matched results.
