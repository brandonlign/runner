- **Reported membership** is the exact frozen label-free P17/P15/P12 halo for each already-discovered primary family.

- P15 `MIN_DIRECTION_NEGATIVES=128` remains immutable; an exact support-poor secondary direction remains unavailable and contributes zero proposals.

The final blind recovery decision is based on the immutable **primary family ranking and exact stable event IDs**. Halo membership may be serialized as characterization, but it cannot independently satisfy recovery if the corresponding primary family did not exist and rank under the frozen core discovery output.
