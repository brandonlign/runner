# Pre-method diagnosis frozen before R1 scientific design

This document records only results that were already exposed before R1 was designed. It is not an R1 performance result.

The frozen v8 matched literature comparison showed a strongly size-dependent gap against full Sugar on exact event-row panels. V8 was approximately tied at the 4–9 annual-member endpoint but materially worse for most 10+ member bins, with especially large deficits for 50–99 and 100+ member showers. The same frozen conclusion found higher dominant-known precision for Sugar than v8.

This pattern motivates exactly one new research question: **can the recurrent fixed4/v8 object be treated as a high-specificity seed rather than the final member set?**

R1 therefore may investigate a final-membership layer, but it may not use the exposed SonotaCo 2023/2025 Sugar/HDBSCAN scores to select thresholds, weights, radii, endpoints, or variants. Those panels remain comparison evidence only.

Previously failed paths remain closed: family reranking, cross-year recurrence-radius variants, component-selection variants, one-to-one/cycle transport, free-form stream tubes, and generic local-likelihood/conformal-density successors. R1 must not silently repackage any of those failures.
