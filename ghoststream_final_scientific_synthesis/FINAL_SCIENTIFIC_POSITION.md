# GhostStream final scientific position

## Final conclusion

GhostStream is a strongly validated candidate meteor stream whose canonical structure is independently recognized by a separately developed, frozen sparse-stream detector. The available evidence rejects an explanation as ordinary structured antihelion background. However, the public data do not currently support a defensible final classification of GhostStream as either a fully distinct stream or a branch related to the nearest catalogue mean, NOP solution 004.

This is the final internally justified scientific position. It preserves the positive results and the unresolved classification boundary without converting either into a stronger claim than the evidence permits.

### 2. Independent methodological recovery

The fixed-4-degree coverage-normalized Mondrian anchored four-clique detector was developed and frozen without using GhostStream values. Its one-shot application in workflow `30927310565` returned `FULL_FROZEN_GHOSTSTREAM_RECOVERY` and passed all 14 frozen gates.

- false-positive rate at alpha 0.01: `0.00703125`.

Every application year from 2022 through 2026 produced nominally significant recovery. This establishes a real connection between the new detector and GhostStream. It is a targeted independent recovery, not the historical discovery method or a complete catalogue-wide blind rediscovery.

### 3. Antihelion and nearest-shower classification

The completed runner classification audit rejected structured antihelion background through the existing source-preserving null evidence. It also established that no complete MDC orbit duplicates GhostStream.

NOP solution 004 is the nearest catalogue mean. The official observation table contains 567 unique observations and reproduces the published radiant, solar longitude, and speed, but does not provide member-level orbital elements.

Exact public-source recovery produced 118 complete orbits from four surveys across eight years. Their medoid reproduces the NOP solution-004 centre extremely well, with `D_SH = 0.02716`. Nevertheless, the prospectively frozen recovery gate failed because the pool contained 118 rather than 150 members, spanned eight rather than nine years, had insufficient recovery from specific sources, and had a member-distance q90 just above the locked 0.35 limit.

A separately frozen branch-versus-distinct static classifier also failed its own control calibration before GhostStream was scored. Both normalized-energy and median-ratio AUROC were `0.7778`, below the required perfect separation across the small frozen control set. Therefore that classifier cannot be used to label GhostStream after seeing the control failure.

## Claims that are supported

1. GhostStream is a statistically and observationally strong meteor-stream candidate.
2. A novel, separately developed frozen detector strongly recovers the canonical GhostStream structure in real local survey backgrounds.
3. The result is not explained by ordinary structured antihelion background under the completed source-preserving null analysis.
4. GhostStream has no complete duplicate among the compared MDC solutions.
5. NOP solution 004 is the closest serious branch alternative and represents a coherent real population.

## Claims that are not supported

1. The fixed-4-degree detector originally discovered GhostStream.
2. The targeted recovery is equivalent to a complete blind catalogue rediscovery.
3. GhostStream is definitively a distinct new stream rather than an NOP-related branch.
4. NOP solution 004 is definitively the same population as GhostStream.
5. Additional internally tuned classifiers or relaxed recovery thresholds can resolve the classification cleanly.

## Remaining external dependency

The only scientifically justified route to a stronger distinct-versus-branch conclusion is one of the following:

- access to the original complete member-level orbit population used for NOP solution 004, including uncertainty information where available; or
- independent expert assessment using non-public or authoritative source data.

Absent that information, further internal variants would be post-result method shopping. The classification uncertainty should be presented as an explicit limitation rather than hidden or forced into a binary answer.

## Recommended project framing

> GhostStream combines a blinded candidate discovery, extensive internal and cross-survey validation, and a separately developed sparse-stream detector that independently recovered the candidate under a frozen protocol. Source-preserving tests reject ordinary antihelion structure, but the candidate's relationship to the nearest catalogue solution, NOP 004, remains unresolved because the complete member-level comparison population is not publicly available.

This framing is strong, coherent, and scientifically honest.
