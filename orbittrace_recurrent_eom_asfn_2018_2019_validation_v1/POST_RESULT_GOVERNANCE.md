# Post-ASFN governance

The ASFN 2018/2019 scientific endpoint is now exposed and binding. ASFN is not available as pristine validation for any successor designed after this result.

Do not use the ASFN endpoint to choose successor mechanisms, thresholds, weights, features, HDBSCAN settings, year handling, ranking, or fusion. Future methodology work must remain on the permanent target-excluded GMN 2022+2023 development split and be independently motivated before outcome.

Separately frozen AMOS 2023/2024 remains untouched and must not be amended based on ASFN.

Protected OrbitTrace `[20°,55°]`, target information/events, MAARSY, and DMS remain inaccessible.
