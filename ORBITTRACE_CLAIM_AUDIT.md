## Final claim set

The current paper may make the following combined claim without exceeding the evidence:

> OrbitTrace is an uncatalogued recurrent late-April observational concentration and meteor-stream candidate discovered blindly in GMN and supported by untouched-year recurrence, source-preserving activity and orbital tests, uncertainty and sampling robustness, geographic/specification checks, candidate-level external archive support, and a separately frozen targeted fixed-4° recovery. Separately, the frozen target-excluded recurrent-EOM HDBSCAN method outperformed the tested Sugar-core and catalogue-HDBSCAN implementations on all four matched-capacity GMN panels and performed positively on an exposed SonotaCo benchmark. Fixed-scale native TopoModal materially improved sparse-sample recovery and dominant precision within GMN. Cross-survey transport of recurrent-EOM was mixed, so pristine or universal method generalization is not claimed.

## Closure rule

For the current paper, no new method search, target-informed tuning, gate relaxation, replacement external-survey search, or post-negative rescue is authorized. New scientific work may be proposed only as a future study with a genuinely new preregistered question; it cannot retroactively change the claims above.
