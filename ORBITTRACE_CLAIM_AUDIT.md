## Final equal-temporal-information literature result

All methods received the same pooled 2013+2014 label-free SonotaCo rows for the relevant pairwise route before any shower truth was loaded. Outputs were frozen first; year-specific truth was then evaluated with the same one-to-one Hungarian-F1 semantics and a candidate budget equal to the comparator's complete returned catalogue.

| Comparator route | Year | Budget | recurrent-EOM macro-F1 / recovered | literature macro-F1 / recovered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| frozen uncertainty-aware Sugar reconstruction | 2013 | 40 | **0.393771 / 24** | 0.272745 / 17 |
| frozen uncertainty-aware Sugar reconstruction | 2014 | 43 | **0.428024 / 24** | 0.293790 / 16 |
| published-configuration catalogue HDBSCAN | 2013 | 14 | **0.220475 / 13** | 0.202064 / 12 |
| published-configuration catalogue HDBSCAN | 2014 | 14 | **0.234838 / 12** | 0.209606 / 11 |

The HDBSCAN gains are modest; the Sugar gains are larger. All four panels satisfy strict macro-F1 superiority and no recovered-shower loss. SonotaCo remains an exposed benchmark, so this supports a fair benchmark superiority claim, not pristine cross-survey validation.

## Final claim set

The current paper may make the following combined claim without exceeding the evidence:

> OrbitTrace is an uncatalogued recurrent late-April observational concentration and meteor-stream candidate discovered blindly in GMN and supported by untouched-year recurrence, source-preserving activity and orbital tests, uncertainty and sampling robustness, geographic/specification checks, candidate-level external archive support, and a separately frozen target-free catalogue recovery. The blind locked-RRF scan recovered a 39-event recurrent family at rank 46 of 766 with 29 exact canonical OrbitTrace members across four years, satisfying its preregistered recovery criterion. Separately, the frozen target-excluded recurrent-EOM HDBSCAN method passed its ordinary-EOM GMN development gate and outperformed the frozen Sugar and catalogue-HDBSCAN comparators on all four panels of an equal-temporal-information pooled SonotaCo benchmark. Fixed-scale native TopoModal materially improved sparse-sample recovery and dominant precision within GMN. Cross-survey transport of recurrent-EOM was mixed, so pristine or universal method generalization is not claimed.

## Closure rule

For the current paper, the blind reveal and equal-temporal-information literature benchmark are complete. No new method search, target-informed tuning, locked-RRF reranking, gate relaxation, replacement external-survey search, or post-negative rescue is authorized. Remaining work is manuscript/submission integration and presentation QA only. New scientific work may be proposed only as a future study with a genuinely new preregistered question; it cannot retroactively change the claims above.
