# OrbitTrace fixed dataset regime

This note freezes dataset roles before any downstream scientific value is opened.

- Development/training: GMN 2022/2023 only.
- Single matched literature-comparison test: SonotaCo 2013/2014 only.
- No-retuning external validation: MAARSY 2022 only.
- Final target-containing search: only after the frozen literature and validation gates pass.

These roles may not be swapped, reused for one-off experiments, or changed after observing outcomes. SonotaCo 2013/2014 and MAARSY 2022 scientific values remain inaccessible during GMN method selection and transport-equivalence work.
