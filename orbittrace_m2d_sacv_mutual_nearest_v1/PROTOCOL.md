## One scientific change

Start from the exact frozen SACV v1 admissible annual hypotheses and exact frozen reciprocal validation relation used by recurrence-first/edge-consensus development.

For each annual hypothesis node, among its already-validated cross-year neighbors choose the unique nearest neighbor under the unchanged SACV physical recurrence distance `d`; deterministic exact ties are broken by the existing stable hypothesis identifier only. Retain an edge iff its endpoints choose each other. No new radius, threshold, support gate, excess gate, contamination convention, null calibration, physical metric, or score is introduced.

Membership semantics are the same conservative correspondence semantics already frozen in #1412, but evaluated only on retained reciprocal-nearest validated edges: the output membership is the union, over retained edges, of the endpoint annual-membership intersections. If no reciprocal-nearest validated edge exists, retain the original M2D parent exactly, matching the inherited fallback convention.

No component ranking, k-core, bridge cut, edge-count threshold, degree threshold, sweep, selector grid, or target-informed rule is allowed.
