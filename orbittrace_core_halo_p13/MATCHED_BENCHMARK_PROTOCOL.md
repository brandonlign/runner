## Panel-specific P13 construction

For each comparator/year pairwise universe independently:
1. Restrict the SonotaCo rows to the exact comparator row universe before any detector object, truth label, score, family, or evaluation is formed.
2. Apply the exact target-independent promoted architecture to that pairwise universe with no retuning. Freeze the panel-specific recurrent **core** families and their exact structural/multiplicity ranking before any truth/comparator outcome access.
3. Run the exact promoted halo architecture, if scientifically defined on that panel, only after the core/rank is frozen; freeze all halo IDs and hashes before truth. The halo can never change a core family ID, core event set, or discovery rank.
4. Primary matched-discovery evaluation uses only the frozen cores and their frozen ranks. Halo membership quality is reported separately and cannot enter the primary comparator score, family match, rank, or superiority decision.
5. Known-shower truth and comparator assignments are opened only after the complete candidate-method core/halo payloads and hashes are frozen.

If the exact promoted halo architecture is not transportable to a pairwise panel without adding an unfrozen modeling choice, that panel is a benchmark incompatibility; no panel-specific rescue/retuning is allowed. The core benchmark may not silently substitute a different halo architecture.

## Exact superiority bars

All bars below are evaluated **pairwise** against one comparator on that comparator's exact-row universe, and the stated condition must hold separately in both 2023 and 2025.

### `BROAD_CATALOGUE_SUPERIORITY`
- core-family macro F1 >= comparator macro F1 + 0.05;
- no nonempty frozen size stratum worse by >0.05 F1;
- >=0.10 F1 gain in at least two frozen size strata per year;
- count of showers with F1>0.5 not lower than comparator;
- every common-universe/integrity/firewall gate passes.

### `SPARSE_STREAM_SUPERIORITY` — mandatory minimum
- 4–9-member mean F1 >= comparator +0.10;
- combined 4–24-member mean F1 >= comparator +0.10;
- overall macro F1 no more than 0.10 below comparator;
- retain >=80% of comparator's count of showers with F1>0.5;
- every common-universe/integrity/firewall gate passes.

P13 is promoted beyond matched literature only if `SPARSE_STREAM_SUPERIORITY` (or the stronger broad bar) passes **separately against Sugar and HDBSCAN in both years**. A win against one comparator/year cannot compensate for failure against another.

## P13 dual-output fairness boundary

P13 receives no credit merely for reporting two layers:
- cores alone define candidate catalogue families for primary discovery comparison;
- exact same core evaluator/matching semantics are applied to comparator clusters on the pairwise universe;
- halos cannot merge/split/rerank cores or supply additional overlap to primary F1;
- halo macro/large-shower membership metrics remain a separate secondary characterization panel and must preserve the P13 development membership-quality gates;
- no maximum-over-core/halo selection, oracle choice, or per-shower layer selection is allowed.

The benchmark claim is therefore about a target-free catalogue-generating core detector, with an independently reported characterization halo, not the better of two outputs chosen after truth.
