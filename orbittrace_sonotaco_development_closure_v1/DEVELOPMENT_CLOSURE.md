## Why development stops here

The exposed SonotaCo sequence has already tested a wide range of independently frozen mechanisms around the v31 boundary, including covariance/metric changes, density-normalized geometry, leave-one-group reference pools, prototype/group geometry, support and recurrence targets, cross-route transfer, component/quality placement, topology, diversity, fusion algebra, continuous exemplar quality transfer, robust scaling, and multiple mechanism-only diagnostics.

Negative outcomes were preserved rather than rescued. Recent binding results further close the remaining obvious local-geometry explanations:

- v57 joint-recurrence geometry: 2/4, no fixed-budget HDB leverage;
- v58 nearest strict-group mean distance: 2/4, HDB worsened to `7` and `6` recoveries;
- v59 continuous 1-NN quality transfer: 2/4, HDB worsened to `7` and `7` recoveries;
- v60 robust-MAD scaling: 0/4, all four panels worsened, HDB fell to `6` and `5` recoveries;
- post-v59 same-route reference-interference diagnostic: FAIL in both years, so cross-route nearest-reference interference is not the general missed-recoverable mechanism.

The repeated pattern is scientifically informative: broad or aggressive rerankings damage an already-strong v31 head, while conservative changes often fail to move the one or two tiny-budget HDB boundary substitutions needed. Continued SonotaCo-specific successor generation would increasingly select ideas because of prior exposed outcomes rather than independent scientific motivation.

## Frozen stopping rule

Effective immediately after this memo is merged:

1. **No new successor may be motivated by SonotaCo 2013/2014 outcomes, missed identities, surfaced identities, boundary ranks, annual panel scores, literature gaps, or post-result SonotaCo diagnostics.**
2. Do not create v61+ by changing weights, thresholds, feature subsets, metrics, scaling, k, annual combiners, diversity, fusion, route handling, component handling, topology, rank windows, budgets, identities, or other choices because they appear likely to improve the exposed SonotaCo panels.
3. Do not run additional SonotaCo diagnostics whose purpose is to identify another rescue axis for the current v31-v60 family.
4. Do not choose among multiple future methods using SonotaCo performance.
5. Preserve v31 as the strongest current defensible SonotaCo development method unless a later method is independently developed outside SonotaCo under the rule below.
6. Every existing failed result remains permanent. Nothing in this closure reopens v32-v60 or any predecessor no-go.

## Only permitted future methodology path

A future successor is permitted only if its scientific mechanism is developed and selected **without using SonotaCo 2013/2014 outcomes**.

The preferred development source is target-excluded GMN development data under the existing OrbitTrace scientific firewall. The protected solar-longitude region `20°–55°` must be removed before labels, reservoirs, windows, scores, folds, endpoints, model selection, diagnostics, or any other development operation.

A future method must be:

- motivated by non-SonotaCo evidence;
- fully specified and frozen before any new SonotaCo execution;
- evaluated on SonotaCo, if used at all, only as an **exposed matched literature benchmark**;
- never tuned or rescued after its SonotaCo result;
- accompanied by the same permanent-failure rule if it does not pass.

Because SonotaCo has already been repeatedly exposed at the project level, even a future independently developed method's SonotaCo result remains development-benchmark evidence, not pristine external validation.

## Interpretation

Failure to force a 4/4 SonotaCo result is not itself evidence that the method is scientifically poor. At the HDBSCAN budgets (`11` in 2013 and `9` in 2014), one family changes the recovery comparison materially. The correct response to repeated exposed-benchmark near-misses is to stop optimizing the benchmark, not to continue until chance or accumulated benchmark knowledge yields a nominal win.

This memo is a scientific-governance freeze. Changing or removing it to permit more SonotaCo-driven successor hunting would itself be a methodology change and must be treated as such in the permanent project record.
