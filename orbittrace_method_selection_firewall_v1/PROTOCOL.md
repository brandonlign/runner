# OrbitTrace target-free methodology succession firewall v1

## Purpose

Freeze the order in which already-defined target-free candidate methods may be evaluated and promoted before any pending v6, P1, P2, matched-literature, external-transfer, or target-containing result is known. This prevents outcome-dependent method selection from being mistaken for a single preregistered discovery method.

Solar longitude 20°–55° remains inaccessible throughout this selection chain. OrbitTrace coordinates, identity, members, previous target rank, and target-containing outputs are forbidden inputs.

Promoted predecessor: v8 pooled-year-centroid label-free sparse-support multiplicity, exact source commit `c9d6c44704013ba0c9430100e98a29a56b453304`.

- 226 recurrent families;

- top-100 dominant precision = 0.6884631112636006.

v8 remains the promoted method unless a successor passes its own frozen development, literature-comparison, and generalization requirements.

## Candidate order

### 1. v3-primary catalogue v6

- `PASS_V3_PRIMARY_CATALOGUE_V6_DEVELOPMENT`;
- `FAIL_V3_PRIMARY_CATALOGUE_V6_DEVELOPMENT`;
- technical/integrity no-result.

A technical no-result may be repaired without changing scientific choices. It may not be reclassified as a scientific failure.

If v6 development passes, v6 must proceed next to the already-frozen pairwise Sugar/HDBSCAN matched-literature adjudication. P1 may not run merely because v6 appears inconvenient or slow.

If v6 development scientifically fails, activate exact frozen P1.

If v6 development passes but the matched-literature classification is `NO_LITERATURE_SUPERIORITY`, activate exact frozen P1 for the project superiority objective. The v6 result remains preserved and may still be reported as an internal methodological result.

If v6 achieves `BROAD_CATALOGUE_SUPERIORITY` or `SPARSE_STREAM_SUPERIORITY`, do not run P1 as a competing alternative before completing v6's frozen no-retuning generalization/transfer stage. A valid v6 generalization success retains v6 as the selected method for the corresponding claim boundary.

A v6 generalization scientific failure may motivate moving to P1; it cannot trigger v6 retuning.

## No best-of-three selection

The project may not run v6, P1, and P2 and then choose the one with the numerically highest GMN, Sugar, HDBSCAN, transfer, or target score.

Selection follows the succession tree above. A predecessor that satisfies the required claim gates remains selected instead of being displaced by an unneeded later method. Later candidates exist as preregistered successors to specific earlier failure modes, not as post-hoc contestants.

## Technical acceleration boundary

Execution-only acceleration is allowed when it preserves the exact scientific computation. Examples include:

- parallel target-excluded years;
- contiguous exact-rescore multiprocessing;
- deterministic center or proposal-record sharding;
- checkpoint/replay from SHA-pinned pre-exact state;
- deterministic batching of the exact D_SH comparator;
- direct finalization from an already-authoritative captured proposal realization when replay proposal regeneration is technically nondeterministic.

An acceleration may not change proposals, event windows, exact distances, floating-point scientific formulas, thresholds, tie rules, components, memberships, family construction, ranking, gates, labels, or blind boundaries. Where an execution partition changes, bounded exact-equivalence or exhaustive hash/order identities must be demonstrated before the accelerated output is used scientifically.

Technical failures and non-reproducible execution plumbing do not authorize scientific revisions.

## Literature standard

Any candidate claiming superiority must satisfy its frozen pairwise adjudication independently against HDBSCAN and Sugar on each comparator's exact event universe. Cross-comparator denominator mixing is forbidden.

The already-frozen broad/sparse standards are not weakened for later candidates.

Input ineligibility caused by immutable support requirements is reported as such and cannot be rescued after outcome by padding events, widening windows, lowering episode sizes, changing quality cuts, or switching core detectors.

## Generalization standard

A matched-literature win is not sufficient for target deployment. The selected candidate must next satisfy its frozen no-retuning generalization/transfer requirement, with the claim strength reflecting whether the external panel is pristine or only architecture-pre-frozen.

If the available external evidence is insufficient for the final target claim, the project must state that limitation rather than infer success from the target itself.

## Final target boundary

No method in this succession tree may access solar longitude 20°–55° until the selected method has completed the required target-excluded development, literature adjudication, and generalization stages and a separate final blind target protocol has frozen:

- exact target-containing input universe;
- complete catalogue ranking output;
- success threshold;
- reveal sequence;
- claim boundary;
- failure interpretation.

The historical v8 partial target recovery is evidence only and cannot select a method, parameter, gate, or target threshold.

## Further successors

If P2 is scientifically rejected, a P3 or later architecture is not automatically authorized. A genuinely distinct successor may be proposed only from aggregate target-excluded failure diagnostics, must freeze its architecture and gates before execution, and must enter a new version of this succession firewall before any scientific run. Nearby threshold/weight/radius variants of rejected methods are forbidden.
