## Frozen matched-capacity rule

For comparator `m` and year `y`:

1. Let `K(m,y)` equal the complete candidate count in the already-sealed literature comparator catalogue.
2. Keep the comparator catalogue unchanged and evaluate all `K(m,y)` candidates.
3. Truncate the immutable local-trunk catalogue to exactly its first `K(m,y)` fixed-rank slots. No successor membership or order is changed.
4. Evaluate both catalogues against the same annual eligible-shower truth with the exact one-to-one Hungarian shower-F1 semantics from the direct literature benchmark.
5. A pairwise win requires local-trunk macro-F1 to be strictly greater than the comparator and local-trunk recovered showers with assigned F1 > 0.5 to be at least the comparator count.

The four primary panels are:

- deterministic published Sugar-core transfer, 2022;
- deterministic published Sugar-core transfer, 2023;
- published-configuration catalogue HDBSCAN transfer, 2022;
- published-configuration catalogue HDBSCAN transfer, 2023.

A full PASS requires all four pairwise gates.

## Interpretation boundary

A 4/4 PASS supports matched-capacity superiority of the frozen local-trunk method to these tested literature implementations on target-excluded GMN. It does not prove universal literature superiority, does not convert the deterministic Sugar core into the full uncertainty-resampled Sugar pipeline, and does not establish cross-survey generalization.

The already-observed recurrent-EOM matched-capacity 4/4 PASS remains historical parent evidence and is not sufficient by itself to award this successor a literature-superiority claim. The successor must pass this direct audit on its own frozen memberships.
