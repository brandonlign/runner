## Architectural inconsistency addressed

Exact P3/P4/P5 currently use one probability system to define a family-direction's reliability/seed floor and another to score its candidate members:

1. a deterministic five-fold **family-excluded** model scores the held-out recurrent seeds and local background and freezes `seed_floor` / `negative_tail`;
2. after those gates are frozen, a separate **all-family** P2 model is fit;
3. candidate probabilities, odds, and cross-family responsibility competition are then computed with that all-family model.

Thus the numerical probability compared with a held-out family seed floor does not come from the model that generated that seed floor. The conflict odds are also not out-of-family for the family they support.

Pretruth-only rescoring showed that the already-surviving P5 proposals also clear their corresponding fold seed floors, so P8 is not motivated as a simple threshold tightening. The scientific question is whether **candidate scoring and cross-family competition themselves** improve when every family's evidence is genuinely out-of-family and on the same calibration system as its held-out recurrent seeds.

## Sole P8 scientific change: consistent crossfit candidate scoring

During the existing five-fold crossfit, retain the exact runtime scaler/classifier pair for each held-out fold.

For every family-direction:

1. identify its deterministic family fold `k`;
2. use only the scaler/classifier trained on families in the other four folds—the same model that generated that direction's frozen held-out seed scores, seed floor, and negative-tail statistic;
3. score every local candidate negative for that family-direction with that family-excluded fold model;
4. require the exact unchanged P3 reliability gate;
5. require candidate fold-model probability >= the exact frozen seed floor generated by that same fold model;
6. require exact P5 joint held-out-seed support in `(d_obs, d_orb)`;
7. convert that same fold-model probability to odds `p/(1-p)`;
8. run the exact unchanged unit-background cross-family responsibility competition and require responsibility >0.5.

A proposal record must freeze the scoring fold identity. Every surviving proposal must prove that its score-model fold equals both the family's deterministic fold and the reliability record's held-out fold.

No all-family-model candidate probability is eligible for P8 membership.
